Privacy Policy

Doodle Break Blocks by vol1mlabs
Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Doodle Break Blocks (the "Game", package name com.vol1mlabs.doodlebreakblocks), developed by vol1mlabs ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the online leaderboard.

1. Summary

  • You do not need to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • The Game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking.
  • Most data (your scores, settings, progress and saved game) stays only on your device.
  • Only the online weekly leaderboard sends a small amount of data to our service provider, Unity Gaming Services.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone and is never sent to us: your best score, level progress, unfinished game, selected theme, language, sound, music and vibration settings, and your profile picture and color choice. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data removes this information.

3. Data used for the online leaderboard

To show the weekly leaderboard, the Game uses Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication and Unity Leaderboards), provided by Unity Technologies. When the Game connects to the internet, the following is processed:

DataPurposeVisible to other players
Anonymous player ID (created automatically, not linked to your identity)Identify your leaderboard entryNo
Nickname (randomly generated, or the one you choose)Show your entry on the leaderboardYes
Your best score of the current week (Classic mode)Rank players on the leaderboardYes
Chosen profile picture and colorShow your profile picture on the leaderboardYes

Like any online service, Unity may also process technical data such as your IP address and basic device information to deliver the service and keep it secure. Please choose a nickname that does not reveal personal information. Unity's privacy practices are described at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

4. How we use the data

We use this data only to run the leaderboard. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or share it with anyone other than Unity, which processes it on our behalf.

5. Data retention

The leaderboard resets every week (Monday 00:00 UTC). Your anonymous player ID and nickname are kept while you use the Game, and you can ask us to delete them at any time (see section 7).

6. Permissions

The Game only uses the Internet permission, for the leaderboard. Everything else works offline.

7. Your choices and data deletion

  • You can play fully offline. Nothing is sent while the device is offline.
  • You can change your nickname at any time from the Profile screen.
  • To delete your leaderboard data, email us at vol1mlabshelp@gmail.com with your nickname. We will delete it within 30 days.
  • Uninstalling the Game removes all data stored on your device.

8. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The leaderboard only uses an anonymous ID, a nickname and scores. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a nickname, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

Data is sent to Unity Gaming Services over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at vol1mlabshelp@gmail.com.
